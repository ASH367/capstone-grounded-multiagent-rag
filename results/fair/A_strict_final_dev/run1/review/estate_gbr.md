## Full case as the system saw it (facts)
[1] On June 2, 2009, the date of the ambulance transport at issue, the beneficiary was an 81-year-old male who had been found unresponsive in his home that morning.
[2] The ER physician documented that he consulted with the neurosurgeon on call, who recommended that the beneficiary be transferred to the “St. Francis Neuro ICU.” Id. Advanced Medical Transport of Central Illinois transported the beneficiary from Proctor Hospital to OSF Saint Francis Medical Center (St. Francis) on the afternoon of June 2, 2009. Id. at 8-10. The beneficiary died two days later. Id. at 5.
[3] The facts show that the patient was transported from one hospital to another on June 2, 2009.
[4] In the request for review (admitted into the record as Exh. MAC-1), the appellant (the beneficiary’s son and co-executor of the estate) asserts that the ER physician at Proctor informed him that the beneficiary needed to be transported to the neurological critical care unit at St. Francis, where he could be evaluated by a neurosurgeon. The appellant further explains that the family did not know whether surgical intervention was warranted until after the beneficiary had been evaluated by the neurosurgeon at St. Francis.
