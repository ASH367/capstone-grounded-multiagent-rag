However, the decision to admit a patient is a complex medical judgment which can be made only after the physician has considered a number of factors, including the patient’s medical history and current medical needs, the types of facilities available to inpatients and outpatients, the hospital’s by-laws and admissions policies, and the relative appropriateness of treatment in each setting.

[5] • The availability of diagnostic procedures at the time when and at the location where the patient presents.
