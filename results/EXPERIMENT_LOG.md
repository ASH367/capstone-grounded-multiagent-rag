# Experiment log: every method tried, with outcomes

Task: Medicare coverage decision (COVERED / NOT_COVERED / PARTLY_COVERED) from case facts + retrieved manual passages. Public data only. Outcome labels are mapped from Appeals Council outcomes (favorable / unfavorable / partial) and never appear in prompts.
Sets: dev = 22 cleaned cases (data/cases_v2_clean); held-out = 18 cleaned cases (data/cases_v3_heldout_clean; 7 COVERED, 6 PARTLY, 5 NOT_COVERED, so the always-COVERED guess is 39%).
"Claude subagent runs" = roles played by Claude subagents through saved files (earlier work). "LangGraph runs" = our own code (rag/langgraph_app.py, rag/tree.py) calling a model API or Ollama. Do not mix the two when comparing.
n is small everywhere. Run-to-run spread is about 1 case, so differences of 1-2 cases are noise.

## D. Caveats to carry into the writeup
- Claude subagent runs were not strictly isolated; some subagents made many tool calls.
- Held-out outcome summaries were visible to the researcher when the criteria trees were written; trees were transcribed from manual text only and committed before any run.
- The criteria-tree decision rule was fixed in advance (NO_INFORMATION -> COVERED) and not changed after seeing results.
- Labels come from appeal outcomes; many PARTLY cases turn on liability or notice, which the manual coverage criteria do not explain.
- Several earlier summary claims were corrected: groundedness advantage (not confirmed), "detailed coordinator prompt reduced performance": correct on held-out (about 2 cases worse), no effect on dev; an interim correction that called it "no effect" overall was itself wrong.

- Fair-test builder (rows 22-23): tagging is by keyword and position rules, not a model; rules were checked against the earlier AI leak audit and by reading samples of dev cases only. Residual leakage cannot be removed by any rule: the Council chooses which record sentences to recite, so recited facts lean toward its holding. The blind probe quantifies it.
- The coverage-only key and merits filter were assigned by reading the operative holdings of all 40 decisions after aggregate held-out results were known. Held-out sentences were read during the NO_INFORMATION check (row 21) and when inspecting why m-12-1140 and m-10-1884 came out with little evidence; arm E and any per-arm word floor were chosen after seeing which held-out cases fell out, so they are exploratory.

## 27. Story-keeping cut (DEV ONLY, exploratory; frozen tagger rag/fairtest.py unchanged)
Why: strict arm drops every sentence after the first Council-voice sentence in a paragraph (COUNCIL_PARA), every EVAL sentence, and every FACT_WEAK sentence (record fact with no date/number). Several of these report plain record facts (e.g. 12-1251 "found in bed in the fowler position", "complained of substernal chest pain"; gold_cross "weak but could stand and walk"). Multi flags mostly ALL_UNKNOWN because those facts are missing.
Audit (dev, 22 decisions, strict story = 5,996 words): clause-level recovery from reasoning sentences adds only ~+7% (rag/cutvariants.py S/M/W); the big reservoir is FACT_WEAK (153 sentences, 3,708 words), of which 107 pieces / 2,367 words pass a conclusion/law/prior-level filter after reading. Arms built (data/cases_v5/): A_plus (strict + clean weak facts) 5,554 -> 7,512 words on the 23 dev MAIN cases (+35%); A_plusW (+ clause recoveries) 7,800.
Reading notes: recovered pieces are overwhelmingly record facts (history, diagnoses, events, vitals, orders). Leak risks found and filtered: 'weighs in favor', 'does not mean', counterfactuals, 'serious medical event', policy bullets. NOT yet measured: blind-probe leak and multi/single performance on A_plus (needs API credit; dev strict probe is already 91%, so dev cannot detect a small leak increase - disclose).
